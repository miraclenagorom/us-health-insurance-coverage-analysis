# US Health Insurance Coverage Analysis

## Project Overview
This dashboard explores health insurance coverage indicators over time and across US states or geographic entries. The screenshot includes changes in coverage between 2010 and 2015, uninsured-rate trends, employer coverage, marketplace-related measures, average monthly tax credits in 2016, and a five-year forecast visualization.

The goal is to make complex coverage data easier to explore and compare.

## Business Questions
- How did the displayed health insurance coverage measure change between 2010 and 2015?
- How do uninsured-rate changes compare across selected states?
- How do the marketplace-related measures vary by state?
- Which displayed states have the highest average monthly tax credit in 2016?
- What forecast is shown for state-level uninsured-rate changes?
